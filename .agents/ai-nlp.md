**Document Parsing:** OCR integration with layout analysis for structured document understanding  - **Named Entity Recognition (NER):** Extract entities like names, addresses, dates, amounts, document numbers from text  - **Sentiment Analysis:** Determine emotional tone, urgency, and risk indicators in customer communications  - **Document Classification:** Categorize documents into types (ID, bank statement, utility bill, etc.) and risk levels  - **Text Summarization:** Generate concise summaries of long documents for compliance review  - **Semantic Search:** Vector-based similarity search across document corpora for adverse media and sanctions screening  - **Language Detection:** Identify document language for routing to appropriate processing pipelines  - **Custom Model Training:** Fine-tune transformer models on tenant-specific data with privacy-preserving techniques  ---  ## 3.
